VIDYASETU – FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

SCHOLARSHIP FAQs

1.  Which scholarships are shown under Featured Scholarships? Answer:
    Featured Scholarships are the recently added or recently modified
    scholarships, including scholarships with a higher scholarship
    amount, that are currently live.

2.  What is a Live Scholarship? Answer: A Live Scholarship is a
    scholarship that is currently ongoing and open for eligible students
    to apply.

3.  What is an Upcoming Scholarship? Answer: An Upcoming Scholarship is
    a scholarship that is scheduled to open in the future and is not
    currently available for application.

4.  What is an All Scholarship? Answer: The All Scholarships section
    displays all the available scholarships, allowing students to browse
    scholarships without applying eligibility filters based on their
    profile.

5.  What is an Eligible Scholarship? Answer: Eligible Scholarships are
    scholarships that are displayed based on the educational details
    provided by the student, such as their current course, discipline or
    specialization, and year of study.

6.  Do I need to log in to view Eligible Scholarships? Answer: Yes.
    Students need to log in and provide their educational details to
    view scholarships for which they are eligible.

7.  Can I view scholarships without logging in? Answer: Yes. Without
    logging in, students can browse the All Scholarships section, but
    personalized eligibility-based scholarship recommendations are not
    displayed.

8.  How are Eligible Scholarships shown to students? Answer: After a
    student logs in and provides their educational details, the system
    matches those details with scholarship eligibility criteria and
    displays the relevant eligible scholarships.

9.  Why can’t I see the Eligible Scholarships option before logging in?
    Answer: The Eligible Scholarships option is based on the student’s
    educational information. Therefore, it is available only after the
    student logs in and provides the required educational details.

10. Can I see scholarships related to my education without logging in?
    Answer: You can browse scholarships under All Scholarships, but
    personalized eligibility-based results require you to log in and
    provide your educational details.

STUDENT SIGN UP – BASIC DETAILS

11. How can I create a student account? Answer: Click Sign Up and
    complete the registration process by providing your basic details,
    educational details, and verification information.

12. What basic details are required during student registration? Answer:
    You need to provide details such as your full name, initials, email
    address, phone number, date of birth, gender, country, and state.

EDUCATION DETAILS

13. What educational details do I need to provide? Answer: You need to
    provide your current studying education details.

14. Why do I need to provide my educational details?
    Answer: Educational details are used to determine and display the 
    scholarships that match your eligibility.

15. What is Class/Course? Answer: Class/Course refers to the academic
    program or level you are currently studying.

16. What is Discipline/Specialization? Answer: Discipline/Specialization
    refers to your specific field of study or specialization within your
    current course.

17. Why is Year of Study required? Answer: Your Year of Study helps the
    system identify scholarships that are applicable to your current
    academic year or level.

18. How does my education determine scholarship eligibility? Answer: The
    system compares your current educational details with the
    eligibility criteria of available scholarships and displays matching
    scholarships under Eligible Scholarships.

HELP & SUPPORT

19. How can I use Chat with AI? Answer: Hover over Help & Support and
    select Chat with AI. You can ask questions related to scholarships
    and receive assistance from the AI.

20. What type of questions can I ask Chat with AI? Answer: Chat with AI
    can answer scholarship-related questions, such as information about
    available scholarships link.

21. Can I ask Chat with AI about my scholarship application status?
    Answer: No. Chat with AI is intended for scholarship-related
    information and general doubts, not website (VidyaSetu)-related
    queries.

22. Can Chat with AI resolve a website-related issue? Answer: No. For
    website (VidyaSetu)-related issues or concerns, use the Raise a
    Query option.

23. When should I use Raise a Query? Answer: Use Raise a Query when you
    have a specific query or issue that requires assistance, especially
    for website (VidyaSetu)-related concerns.

24. Can I raise a scholarship application-related query? Answer:
    No. Raise a Query is intended for website (VidyaSetu)-related
    queries or concerns, not scholarship application-related queries.

25. What is the Frequently Asked Questions section? Answer: The
    Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) section provides answers to
    commonly asked questions about the scholarship platform and
    application process.

26. Where can I find answers about the application process? Answer: You
    can check the Frequently Asked Questions section under Help &
    Support for commonly asked questions about the application process.

27. What should I do if my question is already answered in the FAQ?
    Answer: Check the FAQ section first to find an answer to your
    question. If your question is not covered, use Chat with AI for
    scholarship-related questions or Raise a Query for website
    (VidyaSetu)-related concerns.

HELP & SUPPORT – QUICK GUIDE

Chat with AI - Use for scholarship-related doubts and general
scholarship information. - Example: “What scholarships are available for
graduates?” - Do not use it for website (VidyaSetu)-related issues.

Raise a Query - Use for website (VidyaSetu)-related queries or
concerns. - Do not use it for scholarship application-related queries.

Frequently Asked Questions - Use it to find answers to commonly asked
questions about the scholarship platform and application process.
